The pilot is pre-study evidence and does not consume the proposed twelve-week project. The dataset audit, frozen protocol, Perceval and MerLin code-path verification, finite-shot check, and fold-zero result are already complete. Weeks one and two therefore begin with replication across all five outer folds. Weeks three and four ablate PCA dimension, circuit design, and measurement choices to select a credible candidate. Weeks five and six test photon loss, phase drift, partial distinguishability, detector effects, and noise-aware training. Weeks seven and eight select and freeze the hardware candidate, preprocessing, shot definition, calibration fields, and evaluation subset. Weeks nine and ten run the remote QPU subset with raw counts and calibration metadata, but only if the prior gates pass. Weeks eleven and twelve replicate on harder data or through an independent evaluation, account for resources, and issue the go-or-stop decision memo. The completed pilot shows that I can execute the workflow; the proposed clock is reserved for evidence that can change the application decision.
